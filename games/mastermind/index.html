Mastermind

Guess the secret 4-color code (duplicates are allowed).
The large circles in a row are your guess. The small 2x2 grid of pegs on the right is your feedback.
Black peg: Correct color & position. White peg: Correct color, wrong position.
Note: The position of the feedback pegs in the 2x2 grid does not correspond to the position of the colors in your guess.
